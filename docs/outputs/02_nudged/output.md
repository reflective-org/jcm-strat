## Reading the plots

- Troposphere now carries ERA5's 2005 meteorology: the global KE has a seasonal cycle (high in
  boreal winter, minimum around day 150), which the Held-Suarez-only run did not have. Jets
  are asymmetric between hemispheres in the day-365 (late December) mean.
- Stratosphere above 150 hPa is unchanged from Phase 1 by construction: still Held-Suarez
  (cold tropics, no polar-night jet). Whether the nudged troposphere's wave forcing does
  anything to it needs the daily vortex diagnostic (issue #17).
- The 150 hPa cutoff leaves no visible discontinuity in the zonal means.
- Cost of nudging: 6% in stepping; the ERA5 target (31 GB regridded, resident in RAM) is the
  real cost, plus a one-off regrid when the runner's padded window differs from the prefetch
  (now aligned in `scripts/pipeline.sh`).
