Stratospheric transport scorecard

Six configurations of the dry JCM model, scored against CLaMS, WACCM6 and ERA5 on age of air, the residual circulation and the polar vortex, 2006–2007.

Key findings

No configuration yet gets both the polar vortex and the age of air right. AL7 comes closest (combined score 0.82, 17 of 21 metrics passed). It fixes the vortex but makes stratospheric air about half a year too young.

  1. The vortex is solved by the extratropical temperature correction. AL7's winter jets match ERA5 in both hemispheres within 1–2 m/s; without the correction (P20b) the southern jet is 8–17 m/s too weak.
  2. The same correction makes the air too young. Mean entry-age bias against CLaMS: P20b −0.14 years, AL7 −0.55. All corrected runs have an upward mass flux at 70 hPa 14–26 % above WACCM6, while P20b matches it, so the faster ageing goes with a stronger lower-stratospheric circulation.
  3. Half the correction (AL7h) gives about half of each effect. Age bias −0.37 years, 70 hPa mass flux 6.9 (P20b 6.4, AL7 7.2 ×109 kg/s), southern 10 hPa jet 67 m/s (P20b 57, AL7 75, ERA5 74). It scores 0.93 with 14 passes: better than every run except AL7, but it does not get the vortex without the young air either. Its northern jet above 10 hPa is the weakest of all runs, probably sampling: only two northern winters.
  4. Keeping the correction out of the subtropics (AL7t) does not help. Starting it at 35–50° instead of 25–35° leaves the ages unchanged (bias −0.54) and weakens the northern vortex at 10 hPa (18 vs 26 m/s in ERA5).
  5. Upwelling at 100 hPa is too weak in every run (0.13–0.18 against 0.40–0.44 mm/s), while the 100 hPa mass flux is close. Nudging the tropical upper troposphere (L0, LAL7) raises it by 18–32 % but also makes the air younger.

The runs

All runs use the same dry model: temperature relaxation to a Jucker-type equilibrium, Hines and Lott-Miller gravity-wave drag, ERA5 nudging of winds and temperature in the troposphere (including the lowest levels), QBO nudging, T63 with 95 levels. They differ in a correction to the equilibrium temperature in the extratropics, which strengthens the polar vortex, and in how high the ERA5 nudging reaches. All runs except P20b restart from P20b at the end of 2004 and run 2005–2007.

Age of air

The entry age counts time since the air was last below 150 hPa, so it measures transport within the stratosphere only. CLaMS (driven by ERA5) and WACCM6 bracket the plausible range; they disagree by about 0.6 years in the extratropics at 55 hPa.

Residual circulation

The transformed-Eulerian-mean residual circulation carries tracers up in the tropics and down at high latitudes. Its tropical upwelling at 100 hPa (the shallow branch) and higher up (the deep branch) set how fast air ages.

Caveats

The two age references disagree. At 55 hPa between 50° and 70°, CLaMS gives an entry age of 4.0 years and WACCM6 3.4 years. The 2-D age error and the tropics-to-midlatitude step are scored against CLaMS; against WACCM6 the runs would look less young in the extratropics.

Restart runs. AL7, AL7h, L0, LAL7 and AL7t ran 3 years of their own circulation on top of P20b’s 15. Earlier tests found 3-year and 20-year ages within about 0.1 years, but the deep stratosphere could drift a little further.

Two years only (2006–2007). Winter jets carry a few m/s of year-to-year variability; jet latitudes are on the model grid, about 1.9° apart.

Reference periods differ. CLaMS is the 2005–2009 mean, WACCM6 entry age 2005–2009, WACCM6 circulation 1996–2014 and ERA5 circulation 1996–2001. ERA5 winds and temperatures are for the same months as the runs.

Next step

Scaling the correction trades vortex against age almost linearly, so its amplitude alone cannot reach both targets. The young air goes with the stronger 70 hPa circulation the correction drives. Getting both needs a change that strengthens the vortex without speeding up the lower-stratospheric branch, or a slower lower branch on top of AL7.

Method and reproducibility

Model: JCM (jax-gcm) dry configuration in the reflective-org/jcm-strat repository, branches phase23-lat-nudging, phase24-al7-taper and phase25-al7-half. Scores: scripts/success_metrics.py; figures: scripts/report_figures.py; this page: scripts/build_scorecard.py. Ages are averaged over 2006–2007, circulation from every 6-hourly frame. References: CLaMS v3.1 driven by ERA5, WACCM6 REF-D1 (age) and histSST (circulation), ERA5.
