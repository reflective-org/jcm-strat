Key findings

Starting the QBO nudging at 50 hPa instead of 90 hPa (QBO30) gives the best score so far: 0.88, with 16 of 21 metrics passed. QBO30 is AL7h with one change. It improves the circulation group and keeps AL7h’s vortex, but the age of air is no better, and the QBO below 30 hPa is now weaker and lags ERA5 by a few months. No configuration yet gets both the polar vortex and the age of air right.

  1. Lighter QBO nudging (Phase 27). QBO30 and QBO10d are AL7h with the QBO nudging loosened in two ways: QBO30 starts the nudging window higher, and QBO10d nudges ten times more gently.
    • Both lower the residual-circulation error (group B 0.88 and 0.90, against 1.05 for AL7h).
    • w* at 50 hPa falls from 0.29 to 0.26–0.27 mm/s, closer to WACCM6 (0.20), and now passes. At 100 hPa it rises a little (0.14 to 0.17–0.18) but is still less than half of WACCM6’s 0.40.
    • The upward mass flux at 70 hPa does not drop (6.90 against 6.80 ×109 kg/s).
    • The tropical entry age is slightly younger than AL7h’s (0.94–0.95 against 1.00 years). The overall age error is unchanged (RMS 0.61 against 0.62 years).
  2. QBO30 keeps the vortex; QBO10d weakens the northern one.
    • QBO30’s jets are within 1 m/s of AL7h’s.
    • In QBO10d the northern jet at 10 hPa drops to 16 m/s and moves to 49°N (ERA5 25 m/s at 62°N), so it scores worse overall (0.99).
    • With 10-day nudging the semiannual oscillation near 1 hPa is weaker and drifts out of phase with ERA5. Below 3 hPa QBO10d’s QBO is almost the same as AL7h’s.
  3. QBO30’s cost is the lowest part of the QBO. At 50 hPa the QBO amplitude drops from 10.6 to 7.0 m/s (ERA5 11.3), and the wind there switches direction 2–3 months after ERA5 does.
  4. The two-year scores flattered the corrected runs (Phase 26).
    • All runs except P20b restart from P20b at the end of 2004, and their air keeps getting younger for years after the restart.
    • Entry-age bias against CLaMS, 2006–07 to 2008–09: AL7 −0.55 to −0.70 years, AL7h −0.37 to −0.47, LAL7 −0.84 to −1.04. P20b, which has run since 1990, holds at −0.14 to −0.16.
  5. The extratropical temperature correction fixes the vortex, and it also makes the air too young.
    • AL7’s winter jets match ERA5 within 1–4 m/s. Without the correction (P20b), the southern jet at 10 hPa is 15 m/s too weak.
    • Entry-age bias over 2006–2010: P20b −0.15 years, AL7h −0.43, AL7 −0.64.
    • Half the correction (AL7h) gives about half of each effect. Moving it poleward (AL7t) does not help the age.
  6. Upwelling at 100 hPa is too weak in every run (0.13–0.18 against 0.40 mm/s), while the 100 hPa mass flux is close to WACCM6’s. Nudging the tropical upper troposphere (L0, LAL7) raises it, but it also makes the air younger, and LAL7 scores worst (1.21).
